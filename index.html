What coaching memory is
Every time the AI coach gives you meaningful feedback, that insight is saved here and injected into every future AI conversation. This means the coach remembers your journey — your tendencies, recurring mistakes, and what you have improved — giving you tailored advice rather than starting from scratch each session.
If you clear memory
The AI coach will lose all context about you. Future sessions will feel more generic until new memories accumulate again — this typically takes several weeks of active use to rebuild.
When clearing makes sense
If you are starting a completely new trading approach, if old memories contain incorrect or outdated information, or if you want the coach to approach your sessions without prior assumptions.
